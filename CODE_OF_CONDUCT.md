# Code of conduct

FAM is a room full of people who disagree with each other. That is the point of it, and it
only works if everybody in the room is safe to be there. This is the short version of how
we keep that true.

## What we expect

**Argue with the idea, not the person.** Take on the strongest version of what somebody
said, not the easiest one. "That's rubbish because" is fine. "You're an idiot for
thinking" is not.

**Assume the person next to you knows something you don't.** The room contains people who
have been making a living from creative work for thirty years and people who arrived in
the industry last month. Both of them are worth listening to.

**No stupid questions, and no sighing at questions.** If somebody asks what a word means,
they are doing the whole room a favour. Anybody who makes a person feel small for asking
will be asked to stop.

**Ask before you photograph or record somebody.** We take photographs at events for the
archive. If somebody says no, that is the end of it, no negotiation, no explaining why
they're wrong.

**Take a no.** About a conversation, a drink, a contact detail, a photograph, anything.

**Don't sell from the floor.** Talk about your work all you like. Recruiting, pitching and
working the room for leads are not what this is for.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, including: comments that demean people for their race, gender,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, age,
nationality, immigration status, religion, or class; unwanted sexual attention; deliberate
intimidation, stalking or following; sustained disruption of a talk; unwelcome physical
contact; and photography or recording of somebody who has asked you not to.

This applies in the room, at the bar, in the pub afterwards, and in any group chat that
comes out of it.

## If something happens

Find Ivan, or any other organiser. You can also email
[fam@andcake.dev](mailto:fam@andcake.dev) at any time, including anonymously from an
address we won't recognise.

You will be believed and taken seriously. We will not tell you what you should have done
differently. We will ask what you want to happen, and where we can, we will do that. If
you want somebody removed from the room, say so. We would rather lose an attendee than
lose you.

We can also help you find a quiet spot, get you a taxi, or just stand with you for a bit.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened, we may have a quiet word, ask somebody to leave for the
evening, or ban them from future events. We do not owe anybody a warning first, and we do
not run a three-strikes system.

Organisers are held to this more strictly than anybody else, not less.
